**Wei Feng (冯伟)** is a Software Engineer at Meta working on PyTorch
distributed training — FSDP2, FlexShard, TorchTitan; data parallelism,
Float8, and low-precision dtypes. Before that he worked on recommender
systems at Meta (Reels — retrieval and responsiveness) and as a Lead
Researcher at Hulu. He received his PhD
from Tsinghua University (recommender systems, advised by Jianyong
Wang), was a visiting scholar at UIUC (with Jiawei Han), and got his
bachelor's in software engineering from Beihang University. This site
collects his notes and personal projects on compilers, LLM inference,
and computer architecture.

## Experience

- **Software Engineer, Meta** (2023 - present) — PyTorch distributed:
  FSDP2, FlexShard, TorchTitan; data parallelism, Float8 and
  low-precision dtypes.
- **Software Engineer, Meta** (Sep 2019 - 2023) — Recommender systems
  for Reels: retrieval and responsiveness.
- **Lead Researcher, Hulu** (2015 - 2019) — Recommender systems and
  data science.
- **PhD, Tsinghua University** (2010 - 2015) — Recommender systems.
  Advisor: Jianyong Wang.
- **Visiting Scholar, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**
  (2013 - 2014) — Computer Science. Advisor: Jiawei Han.
- **Bachelor's, Beihang University** (2006 - 2010) — Department of
  Software Engineering.

## Projects

- **tiny-cc** — a from-scratch C compiler (x86-64) with its own libc, an
  RV32IM backend, and a self-hosting toolchain.
  [github.com/weifengpy/tiny-cc](https://github.com/weifengpy/tiny-cc)
- **tiny-infer** — a minimal, readability-first LLM inference engine with
  hand-written CUDA kernels.
- **tiny-serve** — an OpenAI-compatible HTTP serving layer on top of
  tiny-infer.
- **TPU v1 RTL replication** — a faithful RTL recreation of Google's
  TPU v1, simulation-first.
- **Torch2RISCV** — a workbench for compiling PyTorch models down to
  RISC-V on a PYNQ-Z1 board.
