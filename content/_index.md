Wei Feng is a Software Engineer at Meta working on PyTorch distributed training

* [FSDP2](https://docs.pytorch.org/tutorials/intermediate/FSDP_tutorial.html): parameters, gradients, and optimizer states sharded across ranks, and support for float8 all-gather, and LoRA/QLoRA
* [FlexShard](https://github.com/meta-pytorch/flex_shard): pluggable sharding placements for Muon optimizer and block-wise quantization
* [TorchTitan](https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan): PyTorch-native, one-stop platform for production-ready LLM pretraining

Before that Wei worked on recommender systems at Meta and Hulu

* Reels: user-to-item and item-to-item retrieval, improve responsiveness according to in-session engagements
* Newsfeed: diversity re-ranker to ensure calibrated mixture of topics, text, video, and links

He received his PhD from Tsinghua University, advised by [Jianyong Wang](https://dbgroup.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/wangjy/), was a visiting scholar at UIUC, advised by [Jiawei Han](https://hanj.cs.illinois.edu/). He got bachelor degree in software engineering from Beihang University.
