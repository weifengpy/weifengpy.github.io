This site is a combined homepage and blog. The front page is an index of
[my projects](/#projects), and this posts section is where I will write up
notes as I go.

Expect three recurring themes:

- **Compilers** — I maintain a from-scratch C compiler, and I keep learning
  from how real compilers (and real standards documents) draw their lines.
- **LLM inference** — I build a small inference engine and serving layer of
  my own, and I spend a lot of time checking claims from talks and papers
  against the actual code.
- **Computer architecture** — kernels, accelerators, and the occasional
  FPGA board bring-up.

More about the projects themselves lives on the [About page](/about/).
Posts will be short or long as the material demands; the only promise is
that numbers here come with their sources attached.
